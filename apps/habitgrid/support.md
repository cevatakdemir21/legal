  Something not working, or an idea for what HabitGrid should do next? Write to us —
  the same person who builds the app reads the mail.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.15rem">
  <a href="mailto:{{ site.support_email }}?subject={{ page.app_name | uri_escape }}%20Support"><strong>{{ site.support_email }}</strong></a>
</p>

<p>
  We aim to reply within two business days. It helps if you include the version of
  HabitGrid (shown at the bottom of the app’s Settings screen) and your iOS version.
  Tapping <em>Send Feedback</em> in Settings fills all of that in for you.
</p>

<h2>Common questions</h2>

<h3>My habits aren’t syncing between devices</h3>

<p>
  Check that <em>iCloud Sync</em> is turned on in HabitGrid’s Settings on <em>every</em>
  device — it is off by default. All devices must also be signed in to the same Apple
  Account, and iCloud Drive must be enabled in <em>Settings → [your name] → iCloud</em>.
  The first sync can take a few minutes and needs an internet connection.
</p>

<h3>Where is my data stored?</h3>

<p>
  On your device. If you turn on iCloud Sync, a copy also lives in the private area of
  your own iCloud account, which we cannot read. The
  <a href="{{ '/habitgrid/privacy/' | relative_url }}">Privacy Policy</a> explains this in full.
</p>

<h3>Can I move my data to a new phone, or take a backup?</h3>

<p>
  Yes. Use <em>Export Habits</em> in Settings to save a file, and <em>Import Habits</em>
  on the other device to load it. Turning on iCloud Sync does the same thing
  automatically.
</p>

<h3>I’m not getting reminders</h3>

<p>
  Open <em>Settings → HabitGrid → Notifications</em> on your device and make sure
  notifications are allowed. Also check that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not filtering
  them out.
</p>

<h3>How do I delete everything?</h3>

<p>
  Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device. If you used iCloud Sync, also
  remove the app’s data in <em>Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account
  Storage</em>. See <a href="{{ '/habitgrid/privacy/' | relative_url }}">Deleting your data</a>.
</p>
