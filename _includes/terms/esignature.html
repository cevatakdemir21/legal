Signatures and documents

{{ page.app_name }} lets you place a picture of your signature, stamps, text, dates and marks on a document and produces a flattened copy of it. It does not verify who you are, and it does not add a digital certificate, a trusted timestamp or an audit trail. It does not create a qualified or advanced electronic signature (as those terms are used in laws such as the EU’s eIDAS Regulation) or a cryptographic digital signature.

Whether a document signed this way is valid, binding or accepted depends on the document, the other party and the law that applies. Some documents legally require a particular form of signature, a witness or a notary. Check this before you rely on a signed copy. We are not a party to any document you sign, and nothing in the App or on this page is legal advice.

You are responsible for:

  • having the right to import, edit and share each document you use with the App;
  • the accuracy of everything you place on a document;
  • who you send a signed copy to: once it leaves the App we cannot recall it;
  • protecting your device. Anyone who can use your unlocked device can use the signatures and stamps stored in the App, so keep a passcode on it.
