Checking IDs

{{ page.app_name }} reads the data printed in the barcode of a driver license or ID card, or in the machine-readable lines of a passport, and compares the date of birth and the expiration date with today’s date.

{{ page.app_name }} cannot confirm that an ID is real, and it cannot confirm that an ID belongs to the person holding it. A well-made fake can produce a “Pass”. A real ID can be read wrongly, or not at all. A guest on your Banned list who shows a different ID will not be recognised. Treat the result as one piece of information. Look at the ID and at the person yourself, and make your own decision.

Other people’s information

When you scan an ID, the App saves information about that person on your device, as described in the Privacy Policy. You, not we, hold that information and decide what happens to it. You agree to:

  • scan an ID only where the law that applies to you allows it, and only for a purpose it allows;
  • tell people that their ID is being scanned and what you keep, where the law requires it;
  • keep saved scans, exported files and your VIP and Banned lists secure, and not use them for marketing or sell them;
  • answer requests from people who ask what you hold about them or ask you to delete it;
  • not use the Banned list, notes or any other feature to discriminate unlawfully against anyone.

Some laws limit why a business may scan an ID and whether it may keep what it reads. California Civil Code section 1798.90.1 is one example, and other states, provinces and countries have their own rules. If such a rule applies to you, the App’s settings let you turn off Save History, shorten how long scans are kept, and delete them. Nothing in the App or on this page is legal advice. If you are not sure what you may keep, ask a lawyer.
