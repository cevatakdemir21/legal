Purchases and subscriptions (RevenueCat)

In-app purchases and subscriptions in {{ page.app_name }} are sold by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to keep track of what you are entitled to, so that a purchase you have made is recognised on your other devices and can be restored.

We never receive your payment details. Your card, billing address and Apple ID stay with Apple. We do not see them at any point.

RevenueCat receives:

  • a randomly generated identifier for the installation, used as the subscriber ID, and the identifier for vendors (IDFV) — an Apple identifier that is shared only among apps from the same developer and is reset when you delete them;
  • the App Store transaction receipt, which tells us which product was bought, when the subscription renews or expires, and whether it is active;
  • basic technical context — app version, device model, operating system version, and country derived from the App Store storefront.

RevenueCat, Inc. acts as our data processor and provides protection for this data equal to that described in this policy; see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Managing or cancelling a subscription is done through Apple, in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your device. Apple’s handling of the purchase is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
