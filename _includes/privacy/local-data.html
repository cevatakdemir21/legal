Information stored on your device

Everything you create in {{ page.app_name }} — {{ page.content_description | default: "the content you enter, together with your settings and preferences" }} — is stored locally on your device by the App.

This data is not transmitted to us. We operate no server that receives it, and we have no technical means of reading it. It remains under your control: it is included in your device backups if you have those enabled, and it is removed when you delete the App{% if page.services contains 'icloud' %} (see “Deleting your data” below for how iCloud copies are handled){% endif %}.

{% if page.in_app_export %}

The App also lets you export your data to a file and import it back, so you can take a copy with you or move it elsewhere. The export is produced on your device and goes wherever you choose to send it; it does not pass through us.
