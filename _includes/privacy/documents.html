Your documents, the camera and your photos

{{ page.app_name }} works on files you choose to bring in. This is how each way in is handled:

  • Files, Photos and other apps. A document you pick from the Files app, choose from your photos, open in {{ page.app_name }} from another app or drop onto it is copied into the App’s private storage on your device. The App reads only the items you pick. It does not get access to your whole photo library or to your other files.
  • Camera. The camera is used only when you choose to scan a document, a signature or a stamp. iOS asks for your permission first, and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → {{ page.app_name }} → Camera. Scans stay on your device.
  • Password-protected PDFs. A password you type is used once, on your device, to open the file while it is imported. It is not saved.
  • Text recognition. Pages that have no text of their own, such as scans, are read on your device with Apple’s Vision framework so that you can search them. The recognised text is stored with the document on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Spotlight and Siri. So that you can find a document from the Home Screen search or ask Siri or Shortcuts to open it, the App gives iOS the document’s name, its text, its thumbnail and its dates. iOS keeps this in its own search index on your device, which is protected while the device is locked. Apple handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can turn it off in Settings → Siri & Search → {{ page.app_name }}.
  • Sharing and exporting. When you share, export or drag out a document, the flattened copy goes to the app or person you choose, for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files. It does not pass through us. From that moment the recipient’s own terms and privacy practices apply, and we cannot recall the copy.
  • Widget and Control Center control. They contain no document data. They only start a scan or open the file picker.
