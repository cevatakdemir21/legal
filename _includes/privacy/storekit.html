The App Store and ratings

{{ page.app_name }} may invite you to rate it, and offers a link to write a review. Both are handled entirely by Apple’s App Store. If you choose to leave a review, the review and any name shown alongside it are published by Apple under your App Store account, not by us. We receive only the aggregate, anonymous statistics Apple makes available to every developer — such as total downloads and the average rating — which do not identify you.
