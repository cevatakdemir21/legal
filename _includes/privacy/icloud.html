iCloud sync

{{ page.app_name }} can keep your data in step across your devices using Apple’s iCloud, through the CloudKit framework built into iOS. {% if page.icloud_default_off %}This is off by default and only starts when you turn it on in the App’s settings.{% endif %}

When syncing is on, your data is stored in the private database of your own iCloud account. It is not stored on our servers and it is not shared with us. We cannot see, retrieve or restore it. Apple provides this storage as our processor, and its handling of the data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and your iCloud terms with Apple.

Turning syncing off stops further data from leaving your device. Any copy already in iCloud stays in your account until you remove it, which you can do from Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your device.
