iCloud sync

{{ page.app_name }} can keep your data in step across your devices using Apple’s iCloud, through the CloudKit framework built into iOS. {% if page.icloud_default_off %}This is off by default and only starts when you turn it on in the App’s settings.{% endif %} {% if page.icloud_default_on %}This is on by default for devices that are signed in to iCloud, and you can turn it off at any time in the App’s settings.{% endif %}

When syncing is on, your data is stored in the private database of your own iCloud account. It is not stored on our servers and it is not shared with us. We cannot see, retrieve or restore it. Apple provides this storage as our processor, and its handling of the data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and your iCloud terms with Apple.
