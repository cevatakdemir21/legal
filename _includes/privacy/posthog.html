  • a randomly generated identifier for the installation, which is not your name, email address, Apple ID or advertising identifier;

PostHog Inc. acts as our data processor and provides protection for this data equal to that described in this policy; see the PostHog Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement. We do not use PostHog for advertising, and we do not enable cross-app or cross-site tracking.
